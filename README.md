# Bike Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard analysing bike sales across multiple countries, combining KPI cards, trends and customer segmentation to show how revenue, profit and products perform.

## Business Questions

- Which products sell the most by quantity?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How do sales differ by customer gender and age group?
- Which countries contribute the most sales?
- How have revenue and profit changed over time?

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI cards:** Revenue, Profit, Cost and Year-over-Year % change
- **Top 10 Products by Quantity:** bar chart of best-selling items
- **Product Category Sales by Gender:** stacked bar chart
- **Sales by Age Group and Sub-Category:** bubble chart
- **Sales by Country:** map view
- **Interactive filters:** Year parameter and Country filter

## Key Insights

- Bikes are the top category by revenue ([$2.29M] of [$5.69M] total)
- Accessories such as patches, water bottles and tyre tubes lead sales by quantity
- Adults (35-64) and Young Adults are the largest customer segments
